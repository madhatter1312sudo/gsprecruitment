Laatst gecontroleerd: 2026-09-23. Methode: zie [`README.md`](./README.md).

OT-security-specialisten zijn dunner gezaaid in de Brainport-regio zelf
dan embedded/mechatronica-profielen; deze kaart dekt daarom bewust
Brainport plus breder Nederland, zoals de scope van issue #115 toestaat.

## English summary

OT-cybersecurity employers relevant to this pool are spread across the
Netherlands rather than concentrated in Brainport itself: specialised
security firms (Secura/Bureau Veritas, Northwave, DNV Cyber, Aumatics,
Navaio), a certification body (TÜV Rheinland Nederland) and one
Brainport-based OEM with an in-house crypto-security practice (NXP). The
two vacancies picked as top priority — OT/ICS security architect (senior)
and OT security engineer IEC 62443 (senior) — follow the same
seniority-based first assumption used across the other maps. The
long-list points sourcers to the Cyber Weerbaarheidscentrum Brainport,
the Amsterdam Cyber Security for Control Systems meetup, and a public
Dutch ICS/SCADA/OT conference — all public, non-login sources, no
individuals named.
