## English summary

Employers around Brainport/Eindhoven hiring embedded C/C++ engineers span
OEMs (ASML, NXP, Philips), engineering service providers (Prodrive
Technologies, Sioux Technologies, ICT Group, Alten Nederland) and one
large in-house software team (Vanderlande's Software House in Veghel).
The two vacancies picked as top priority, embedded software architect
(senior) and embedded Linux/Yocto engineer (senior), are the most
senior, specialised roles in the live pool, in the absence of an explicit
ranking field; a recruiter should confirm this with the chief before
relying on it. The long-list points sourcers to TU/e's Master Embedded
Systems, the 040coders and Bits&Chips communities, the High Tech Campus
Software & AI cluster, and the Zephyr/Yocto open-source projects, all
public, non-login sources, no individuals named.
