## English summary

Test and verification employers around Brainport combine one clear
in-house case (Thermo Fisher Scientific's NanoPort test/verification team
in Eindhoven) with engineering service providers whose scope includes
test (Sioux Technologies, ICT Group, Vanderlande's Software House) and
dedicated test specialists (Polteq, Sogeti Nederland, TÜV Rheinland
Nederland). The two vacancies picked as top priority — QA-lead embedded
(senior) and HIL-testautomatiseringsengineer (senior) — follow the same
seniority-based first assumption used across the other maps. The
long-list points sourcers to TestNet (the Dutch software-testing
association), Polteq's own training institute, High Tech Institute, DSPE
and TU/e's Embedded Systems master — all public, non-login sources, no
individuals named. A HIL tool vendor such as dSPACE was considered but
left out for lack of a verifiable Dutch entity page.
