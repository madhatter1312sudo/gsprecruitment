## English summary

Mechatronics and control-software employers around Brainport cluster
around precision manufacturing for semiconductor, medical and analytical
equipment: ASML and OEM-adjacent engineering firms (Prodrive Technologies,
Sioux Technologies) alongside specialised mechatronics suppliers (VDL
ETG, NTS Group, Hittech Group, MI-Partners, IBS Precision Engineering,
Anteryon, Frencken Mechatronics). The two vacancies picked as top
priority — systeemengineer semicon (senior) and mechatronisch
systeemontwerper (senior) — follow the same seniority-based assumption
used across all four maps, pending an explicit ranking from the chief.
The long-list points sourcers to DSPE (the Dutch precision-engineering
society), TU/e's Systems and Control master, the Mechatronics Academy
and the Brainport Industries member list — all public, non-login
sources.
