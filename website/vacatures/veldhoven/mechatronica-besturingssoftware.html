Alongside ASML, headquartered in Veldhoven with heavy mechatronics and motion-control content in its lithography systems, the city hosts MI-Partners: a mechatronics centre of expertise developing high-tech mechatronic products and systems for clients worldwide. Demand ranges from servo control engineering to precision-level system design. We recruit continuously for clients in this chain, anonymous until a first conversation.
